## Baseline (rebuild exactly)
Full-window 3D cube on a dark background, keyboard controlled, with Scramble/Solve/Reset buttons at the top left.
- 27 cubies: near-black bodies with colored stickers inset on the outward faces. Colors: U white, D yellow, F green, B blue, R red, L orange.
- Perspective camera orbits the cube. Dragging (mouse or touch) rotates the view, and the vertical angle is clamped so it never flips. Ambient light plus a directional light that follows the camera. Resizes with the window.
- Keys: U D L R F B turn that face 90° clockwise (as seen looking at that face). X Y Z rotate the whole cube (X follows R, Y follows U, Z follows F). Shift + key reverses the turn. Held-key auto-repeat is ignored.
- Turns animate over about 160 ms with ease in-out. Keypresses queue and play in order. After a whole-cube rotation, face keys refer to whatever face is now in that position. Snap positions and rotations to exact values after each turn so drift never builds up.
- Scramble: 25 random quarter turns, never the same face twice in a row, animated fast.
- Solve: plays back the inverse of every move since the cube was last solved, in reverse order. It is not a real solver. A move followed by its inverse cancels out of the history.
- Reset: instantly restores a solved cube and clears the queue and history.
- A green "Solved" label shows whenever the cube is solved and idle, in any orientation.
- Bottom help line lists the keys and "drag to rotate the view".

## Feature 1: CFOP hints
- A Hints button in the top bar shows its on/off state. The H key also toggles it.
- The hint panel shows: the current stage, the goal of the next step in plain words, the case name if there is one, and the move sequence with the next move marked. The layer for the next move is highlighted on the 3D cube.
- If I play the suggested move, the panel advances. If I play any other move, the hint recomputes.
- Hints are hidden while a timed solve is running.
- Stages are checked in this order (the first one not done is the current stage):
  1. Orientation: white center must be on D. Hint the rotation that puts it there.
  2. Cross: 4 white edges on D, each matching its side center. Hint a move sequence, ideally optimal (8 moves max).
  3. F2L: all 4 corner-edge pairs inserted. Hint the easiest next pair, with moves that don't break the cross or pairs already solved.
  4. OLL edges: yellow cross on U. Hint the case (dot, L, line), any U setup turn, and the alg.
  5. OLL corners: whole U face yellow. Hint the case, setup turn, and alg.
  6. PLL corners permuted: hint the case (adjacent or diagonal swap), setup turn, and alg.
  7. PLL edges, solved up to a U turn: hint Ua/Ub/H/Z, setup turn, and alg.
  8. AUF: the final U turn.
- The app must track sticker colors, not just 3D transforms. The move system must support R2, wide moves (r, f), M, and D, both for logic and for animation.
- OLL/PLL hints must use exactly the algorithms in Feature 2.
- Computing a hint must never freeze the page (use a Web Worker or keep it fast).

## Feature 2: Algorithms tab
Add a top bar tab switch: Cube | Algorithms. The Algorithms tab has one card per case. Each card shows:
- The case name and group.
- A case diagram generated by applying the inverse of the alg to a solved cube, so a diagram can never mismatch its alg:
  - OLL: top-down view of U plus the side stickers of the top layer, yellow vs gray only.
  - PLL: the same view in full color, with arrows for the pieces that swap.
  - F2L: a 3D-looking view of U/F/R with only the cross, the target pair and the centers colored; everything else gray.
- The alg in standard notation.
- A mini animated cube that starts in the case state and plays the alg, with Play/Pause, step back, step forward and Reset controls, and the current move highlighted in the alg text.

Algorithms (20):
- F2L basic inserts (slot front-right, cross on D):
  - Corner UFR white facing front, edge UR: U R U' R'
  - Corner UFR white facing right, edge UF: U' F' U F
  - Corner UFR white facing right, edge UB: R U R'
  - Corner UFR white facing front, edge UL: F' U' F
- OLL edges: Line (hold horizontal) F R U R' U' F' | L (hold at back-left) f R U R' U' f' | Dot F R U R' U' F' f R U R' U' f'
- OLL corners: Sune R U R' U R U2 R' | Antisune R U2 R' U' R U' R' | H R U R' U R U' R' U R U2 R' | Pi R U2 R2 U' R2 U' R2 U2 R | Headlights R2 D R' U2 R D' R' U2 R' | Chameleon r U R' U' r' F R F' | Bowtie F' r U R' U' r' F R
- PLL corners: Adjacent swap (T) R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F' | Diagonal swap (Y) F R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R F'
- PLL edges: Ua R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2 | Ub R2 U R U R' U' R' U' R' U R' | H M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2 | Z M' U M2 U M2 U M' U2 M2
Verify every alg in code: starting from its own case state, it must leave its target (the slot, the U face, or the whole cube) solved, allowing one final U turn. If an alg fails, fix it and tell me which one.

## Feature 3: Timer (Cube tab, behaves like csTimer)
1. Show a 20-move scramble as text and apply it to the cube, either when I trigger a timer scramble or after the previous solve finishes.
2. Optional 15 s WCA inspection, off by default: space starts the countdown. Over 15 s = +2, over 17 s = DNF.
3. Hold space: the timer goes red, then green after 0.3 s. Releasing before green cancels.
4. Releasing on green starts the timer: large digits, 0.01 s precision.
5. The timer stops when the cube is solved (any orientation) or when I press space. Escape stops it as a DNF.
- Session list, newest first: each time with +2, DNF and delete actions, plus its scramble. Show current, best, ao5, ao12 and mean. Averages use WCA rules: drop best and worst; more than one DNF in an ao5 makes it a DNF.
- Save times in localStorage, wrapped in try/catch.
- Face and rotation keys keep working while timing. Space is reserved for the timer. During a timed solve, hints are hidden and Solve/Scramble/Reset are disabled. Releasing space must never trigger a focused button.

## Acceptance checklist
- Baseline behaves exactly as described.

- All 20 algs pass the in-code verification.
- Following hints from 100 random scrambles always reaches solved (automated test).
- No hint freezes the page.
- Each algorithm card's diagram and mini cube start in the same state, and the animation ends with its target solved.
- Timer: a hold under 0.3 s doesn't start it, release on green starts it, solving stops it, +2/DNF/delete update ao5/ao12 correctly, and times survive a reload.
- Space never triggers a button.
